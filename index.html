A clear process

A careful route from problem to next step

Document the issue, use the provider’s official procedures, and assess what can realistically be verified.

01

Describe the incident

Record the account or service involved, the timeline, the error or incident, and what changed.

02

Gather evidence

Keep usernames or account emails, profile URLs, platform notifications, case numbers, screenshots, transaction hashes, wallet addresses, and receipts as relevant.

03

Check reporting routes

Use the platform’s official recovery or support process. For crypto incidents, contact the relevant exchange, wallet provider, payment provider, or appropriate authority.

04

Assess options carefully

Understand what can be verified, what remains uncertain, and never rely on guarantees or anyone offering to bypass platform security.

Prepare your records

Evidence is more useful than promises.

Clear records can help a platform’s official support team or an appropriate professional understand an account-access issue or crypto incident. Never share credentials or wallet secrets to explain what happened.

✓Account username or email address, and the profile or channel URL where relevant.
✓Approximate date access was lost, plus relevant platform notifications or error messages.
✓Screenshots and official platform support or appeal case numbers.
✓For crypto: transaction hashes, sending and receiving wallet addresses, and blockchain network.
✓For crypto: asset and amount, relevant communications, receipts, and payment records.

Before contacting anyone

  • Save original records and keep backup copies.
  • Do not edit screenshots in a way that changes the evidence.
  • For crypto transfers, check transaction details on the appropriate blockchain explorer.
  • For account issues, use the platform’s official recovery and support pages; for crypto, contact your exchange or payment provider promptly.
  • Consider reporting suspected crime to the appropriate authorities.
  • Do not publish personal information or sensitive account details publicly.
✓

Protect your accounts and wallet

Never disclose your seed phrase, private key, password, or one-time verification code to someone claiming to help. Use official platform pages, secure affected email accounts, review active sessions, and do not install remote-access software at a stranger’s request.

Frequently asked questions

Know the risks before you act

Practical answers about account access, official recovery procedures, cryptocurrency incidents, and recovery-scam warning signs.

Can RecoveryLab bypass a platform’s security or guarantee account access?

No. Legitimate account recovery must follow the relevant provider’s official procedures. RecoveryLab is not affiliated with the platforms listed here and does not hack accounts, bypass security, or guarantee that access can be restored.

What should I do if my Gmail, Facebook, Instagram, or other account was hacked?

Start from the platform’s official account-recovery or compromised-account page. Secure the email account linked to the profile, review security notifications, and follow the provider’s identity and verification steps. Do not trust unsolicited support accounts or share verification codes.

Can you recover a Roblox, Epic Games, FC Mobile, or ChatGPT account?

We provide general guidance on documenting the issue and finding official support routes. Each provider controls its own recovery process, and outcomes depend on its rules and the evidence available.

Can stolen cryptocurrency always be recovered?

No. Recovery is never guaranteed. Blockchain transfers are generally irreversible, and the outcome depends on factors such as where the assets went, whether an exchange can identify or freeze them, the available evidence, and applicable law.

Can someone recover funds using only a transaction hash?

A transaction hash can help verify a transfer and trace its on-chain path. It does not grant control over the receiving wallet and does not, by itself, enable anyone to reverse a transaction.

Should I pay a fee to unlock my profits or recover my funds?

Be cautious of demands for taxes, deposits, verification payments, gas fees, or other charges sent to a private wallet before a promised release. Verify the claim independently through official channels. A demand for another payment is a common warning sign of fraud.

What if a recovery company guarantees success?

Treat guarantees with skepticism. Ask for the company's legal identity, qualifications, written scope of work, full fee terms, and independently verifiable references. Verify any claimed regulatory status with the relevant regulator.

What if my wallet or exchange account was compromised?

Use the provider's official website or application to secure your account, change affected passwords, revoke suspicious sessions or approvals where appropriate, and contact official support. If a seed phrase was exposed, consider moving remaining assets to a securely created wallet. Never enter the phrase into a website suggested by a stranger.

What information should I share for an initial discussion?

Start with a brief description, approximate date, asset and network, and whether you have a transaction hash. Avoid sending identity documents, full account records, or other sensitive information until you have independently verified who you are dealing with and why that information is necessary.

Does contacting this website guarantee an investigation or recovery?

No. Contacting this website does not guarantee that a case will be accepted, independently investigated, or resolved. Confirm the operator's identity, qualifications, scope of service, privacy practices, and fees before engaging any service.
